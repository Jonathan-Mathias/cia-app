# Elite Migration — Privacy Policy Draft for CIA App

Status: draft for review
Last updated: 2026-06-04

> This draft is written for the CIA App MVP. It is not legal advice. Before scaling paid products, ads, or high-volume traffic, Elite Migration should review this with a qualified lawyer/privacy professional.

## 1. Who we are

Elite Migration is a career positioning project created by Jonathan Mathias to help qualified Brazilian professionals understand and improve their readiness for international career opportunities, starting with Germany and later expanding to other markets such as Australia.

The CIA App — Career Intelligence Assessment — is an educational diagnostic tool. It does not provide immigration, legal, financial, tax, visa, employment, or migration agent advice.

## 2. What data we collect

When you use the CIA App, we may collect:

- name;
- email address;
- WhatsApp number;
- target destination selected in the assessment, such as Germany or Australia;
- age range;
- current country/location context;
- time living outside Brazil, if applicable;
- assessment answers;
- final score and readiness band;
- score by diagnostic dimension;
- alerts, insights, next steps, and CTA shown to you;
- CTA clicks and interaction events;
- NPS/feedback and optional comments;
- technical metadata such as date/time and source of the interaction.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive documents, passport numbers, official visa files, employer confidential data, or private family information through the CIA App.

## 3. Why we collect this data

We use this data to:

- generate and display your personalized assessment result;
- send your result and relevant next steps by email or WhatsApp;
- understand which career gaps are most common among users;
- improve the CIA App, emails, content, and future Elite Migration products;
- segment users into educational journeys based on readiness level;
- measure product quality, conversion, and demand signals;
- prevent misuse, spam, or technical abuse.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. Educational nature of the assessment

The CIA App is an educational and informational tool.

It does not guarantee:

- a job offer;
- visa approval;
- migration outcome;
- interview invitations;
- salary increase;
- acceptance by companies, recruiters, universities, authorities, or partners.

Any career, immigration, legal, tax, financial, or relocation decision should be made with appropriate qualified professionals.

For Australia-related immigration matters, users should consult a MARA-registered migration agent when needed.

For Germany-related immigration/legal matters, users should consult qualified immigration/legal professionals when needed.

## 5. Tools and service providers

To operate the CIA App, we may use trusted technical providers such as:

- hosting platforms, such as GitHub Pages or future hosting providers;
- database and backend tools, such as Supabase;
- automation and email tools, such as Brevo or Google Apps Script;
- analytics or logging tools used to understand product performance;
- communication tools such as email or WhatsApp links.

These tools may process data on our behalf only for operating the assessment, communication, analytics, or product improvement.

## 6. Partners and external links

The CIA App may show links to products, partners, communities, or external resources.

If you click an external link, the destination website or partner may have its own privacy policy, terms, and data practices. Elite Migration is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

Some partner links may be affiliate or referral links. When that is the case, Elite Migration should disclose it clearly near the relevant link or page.

## 7. Communication consent

By submitting your contact details, you agree that Elite Migration may contact you by email and/or WhatsApp with:

- your assessment result;
- educational content;
- next steps related to your diagnostic profile;
- updates about relevant resources, products, or future services.

You may ask to stop receiving communication at any time.

## 8. Data retention

For the MVP stage, Elite Migration may retain assessment data while the product is being developed and validated.

Data may be kept in identifiable form when needed to send results, follow up, or understand user journeys.

Data may also be used in aggregated or anonymized form for internal learning, product decisions, content strategy, and market understanding.

## 9. Your rights

You may request:

- access to the data we hold about you;
- correction of inaccurate data;
- deletion of your personal data when reasonably possible;
- removal from email or WhatsApp communication;
- clarification about how your data is used.

To request this, contact Elite Migration through the official contact channel listed below.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data and avoid unnecessary collection.

However, no online system is completely risk-free. Users should not submit sensitive documents or confidential information through open forms unless explicitly requested through a secure channel.

## 11. Children

The CIA App is intended for adults and career professionals. It is not designed for children or minors.

## 12. International users

The CIA App may be accessed by users in different countries. By using it, you understand that your data may be processed by tools or providers located in different jurisdictions.

## 13. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the CIA App and Elite Migration platform evolve.

The latest version should be available through the CIA App or Elite Migration official channels.
